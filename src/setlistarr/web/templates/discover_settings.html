ⓘ How the two sources differ
  • Last.fm similar artists - for the artists you play most, Last.fm lists similar artists; ones you don't own are ranked by how many of your artists point to them. Predictable, "more like what I love". Needs the Last.fm API key (Settings → Enrichment) and one Enrich tags run.
  • ListenBrainz recommendations - personal recommendations from your actual listening (like Spotify's), refreshed by ListenBrainz about weekly. Needs a token below, and a few weeks of plays sent to ListenBrainz before the first ones appear.
  • Approving sends the artist to Lidarr with only its most popular album monitored and searched - no whole discographies, no future releases. With auto-approve off (the default) nothing is downloaded until you approve it on the Discover page.
