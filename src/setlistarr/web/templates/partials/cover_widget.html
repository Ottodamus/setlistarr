ⓘ What to expect from each option
  • Artwork - image models (an OpenAI-compatible provider set to an image model such as gpt-image-2.5-flare): illustrated or photo-style art made by OpenAI's Images API. Takes 10-60 s. Billed by OpenAI per image - a few cents for a 1024×1024 image at medium quality (set quality per provider). OpenAI may refuse prompts naming real artists, bands or people. chatgpt-image-latest, gpt-image-1 and gpt-image-1.5 stop working on 2026-12-01.
  • Designed art - text models (Claude, ChatGPT chat models, Ollama): these models can't make pictures, so they draw the cover as vector graphics - bold shapes, gradients, simple type - which Setlistarr renders to an image. Not photographic. Claude costs roughly 3-10¢ per cover (less on Sonnet/Haiku); local Ollama is free but small models make simpler art and sometimes need a second try.
